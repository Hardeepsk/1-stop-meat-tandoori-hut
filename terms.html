Allergies

Our dishes may contain or come into contact with common allergens. If you have an allergy, tell us in the order note and also call us at (519) 668-6064 to confirm before you order. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free of an allergen.

Direct order discount

Orders placed directly on this website get 10% off food.

Prices and totals

Prices, taxes, any delivery fee, your tip and the total are shown at checkout before you place the order. The total we charge is the total confirmed by our ordering server when the order is placed.

Payment

Checkout shows the payment options available for your order: pay by card online, or pay at pickup. Amazon warehouse delivery orders are paid by card when you order. Card payments are processed by Stripe, and we never see or store your card details.

Pickup and Amazon warehouse delivery

Online orders are for pickup at our restaurant. We don't deliver online orders to homes. Amazon warehouse employees can choose free Amazon warehouse delivery: tell us where to bring the order, and we bring warehouse orders together in one bundled run. Pickup and delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.

Changes and cancellations

You can cancel an order online only until the kitchen accepts it. After that, call us at (519) 668-6064. If you cancel an order that you paid for online by card, the full amount is refunded to your card automatically.

Meat counter items

Raw meat from our meat counter is sold as groceries.

Your account and texts

You sign in with your mobile phone number and a one-time code sent by text message. We text you about your order, and you can stop order texts by replying STOP. See our privacy policy for how we handle your information.

Questions

If something is wrong with your order, call us at (519) 668-6064.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. The terms in effect when you place an order apply to that order.
