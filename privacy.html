Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains what personal information 1 Stop Meat & Tandoori Hut collects when you order direct on this website, why we collect it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. We are a restaurant and meat counter at 1070 Wellington Rd, Unit 14, London, ON N6E 1M2.

What we collect

You can browse the menu without giving us anything. If you sign in or place an order, we store:

  • Your mobile phone number, which you use to sign in with a one-time code sent by text message.
  • Your name.
  • Your email address, if you choose to give one.
  • Whether you agree to marketing texts. This is off unless you turn it on.
  • Up to 5 saved addresses, with any delivery instructions you add.
  • Your orders: the items, prices and totals, your order note, how you get the order (including any Amazon warehouse drop-off details you enter), the payment status and the order status history.

To keep you signed in and keep sign-in secure, we also store a scrambled (hashed) version of your sign-in token, which lasts 30 days, and a hashed version of each one-time code, which expires after 5 minutes. We do not keep the codes or tokens themselves in readable form.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and manage your account.
  • To prepare, deliver and take payment for your orders.
  • To text you about your order, such as when it is received, ready, out for delivery or cancelled.
  • To send deals and specials by text, only if you have turned on marketing texts.
  • To keep records of orders for tax purposes.

Text messages

Sign-in codes and order status texts are part of the service. You can stop order texts by replying STOP to the message. We send marketing texts only if you opt in on your account page, and you can turn them off there or by replying STOP.

What stays in your browser

Your browser keeps your cart and your sign-in session in its local storage so they are still there when you come back. This stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Who else handles your information

We use these service providers to run the site and the ordering system:

  • Twilio sends our sign-in codes and order status texts, so it receives your phone number and the text content.
  • Stripe processes card payments. When you pay by card, you enter your card details on a Stripe payment page. Card details go to Stripe and are never stored by the restaurant.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and a cloud hosting provider runs our ordering server and its database.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts this site uses, so your browser contacts Google to load them.
  • Google Maps is loaded only if you press the button to open the interactive map on the home page. Until then, the map is a plain image.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as your account is open. Sign-in tokens expire after 30 days and one-time codes after 5 minutes. When you delete your account, we remove your profile, saved addresses and sign-ins. We keep past orders for tax records, but with your name, phone number, order note and street address removed. Only the first 3 characters of the postal code are kept. We keep daily backups of the ordering system for about two weeks, so deleted details can stay in those backups until the older copies are replaced.

Delete your account

Sign in, open your account page and choose “Delete my account”. You can also call us at (519) 668-6064 and ask us to do it.

Your rights and questions

Under Canada’s privacy law (PIPEDA), you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Call us at (519) 668-6064 to make a request. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
